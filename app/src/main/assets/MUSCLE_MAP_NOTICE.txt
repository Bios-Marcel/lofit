Muscle map artwork

Anatomical paths adapted from react-native-body-highlighter:
https://github.com/HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter
Commit: 8ed39ac2ae9cb46fb79d77eedec7e5b029a75174
Source files: assets/bodyFront.ts, assets/bodyBack.ts,
assets/bodyFemaleFront.ts, assets/bodyFemaleBack.ts,
components/SvgMaleWrapper.tsx, components/SvgFemaleWrapper.tsx.

Paths are stored in res/raw/muscle_map_front.json and muscle_map_back.json.
Female paths are stored in res/raw/muscle_map_female_front.json and
muscle_map_female_back.json.
SVG command spacing is normalized for Android PathParser. Colors, shading,
viewport, and exercise muscle-group mappings are specific to Lofit.
